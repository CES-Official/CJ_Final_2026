Boilerplate

CyberLearning Educational Society is a not-for-profit organization committed to advancing digital readiness among school students. Its mission is to equip young learners with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to navigate the digital world safely, think critically, and use technology responsibly. The Society brings together educators, industry experts, and academic leaders to design and deliver impactful learning initiatives that prepare students for an AI-powered future.

The Society organizes COMPUDON Junior, India's championship for future-ready digital skills, providing schools with a structured platform to develop and recognize student achievement in AI awareness, digital citizenship, cyber safety, media literacy, and critical thinking. COMPUDON Junior is aligned with national and international frameworks, including NEP 2020, NCF 2023, CBSE Skill-81/2025, UNESCO's Media and Information Literacy (MIL) framework, and the OECD Learning Compass 2030.
