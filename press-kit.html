About CyberLearning Educational Society

CyberLearning Educational Society (CES) is a not-for-profit organization committed to advancing digital readiness among school students. CES works to equip young learners with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to navigate the digital world safely, think critically, and use technology responsibly.

The Society brings together educators, technology professionals, and academic leaders to develop learning initiatives that help students build practical digital skills for an increasingly AI-powered world.

CES is the nonprofit arm of India CyberLearning Pvt. Ltd., a Microsoft Global Training Partner, and is also a partner of the National Digital Literacy Mission (NDLM).

About COMPUDON Junior

COMPUDON Junior is a national championship on digital literacy skills for students ages 8–14.

The championship gives students an opportunity to develop and demonstrate practical skills in digital literacy, cyber safety, AI literacy, media literacy, creativity, and critical thinking.

COMPUDON Junior combines learning, assessment, competition, and recognition, giving students an opportunity to apply what they learn and demonstrate their skills at a national level.

The championship was launched in 2019 and has attracted students from schools across India. Its earlier seasons drew several thousand contestants from around the country.

COMPUDON Junior builds on the experience of COMPUDON, India's first official championship on Microsoft Office applications, launched in 2011. COMPUDON created a pathway for Indian school students to compete at the Microsoft Office Specialist World Championship.

Why We Do This

Children are growing up in a world shaped by AI, social media, misinformation, cyber threats, and rapidly changing technology.

Digital access alone is not enough. Students need to know how to use technology safely, evaluate information critically, recognize risks, and make responsible decisions.

COMPUDON Junior exists to help schools build these capabilities in a structured, measurable, and age-appropriate way.

Founder

PANKAJ RAI

Pankaj Rai is the Founder and President/CEO of CyberLearning Educational Society and the founder of COMPUDON.

He has worked in technology education and digital literacy for more than two decades, with initiatives that have reached more than 1 million learners and professionals.

In India, Pankaj established a network of more than 1,000 testing centers for professional certification examinations. He also helped introduce globally recognized certifications including Microsoft Office Specialist, Microsoft Technology Associate, Adobe Certified Associate, HP ATA, and IC3.

A 2009 interview with Elets digitalLEARNING identified Pankaj Rai as Director & CEO of CyberLearning and documented the company's work in global technology certifications. At the time, Certiport had appointed CyberLearning as the exclusive provider for certain Microsoft Office, Adobe and digital literacy certifications for the Indian subcontinent.

Internationally, as Chief Operations Officer of the National Education Foundation for Cyberlearning, USA, Pankaj was involved in the implementation of the Universal ICT Education initiative in Mauritius. In 2006, the Government of Mauritius approved a four-year action plan to train 400,000 people in IC3, designated as the official certification for digital literacy in Mauritius. The government record identifies Dr. Appu Kuttan, Founder, Chairman and CEO of the National Education Foundation for Cyberlearning, in connection with the initiative.

As COO of the National Education Foundation, Pankaj also established international partnerships and initiatives across China, Singapore, Egypt, the Caribbean, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, and other countries.

Through COMPUDON, Pankaj created a pathway for Indian students to compete internationally. COMPUDON students have achieved top honors at the Microsoft Office Specialist World Championship, including India's first Microsoft Office Specialist World Champion in PowerPoint and World Championship runner-up positions. Independent school records document Arjit Kansal as India's first Microsoft Office Specialist World Champion and Shashank Batra as a silver medalist at the 2014 World Championship.

Pankaj continues to focus on building practical, scalable digital education initiatives that help young people participate safely, confidently, and responsibly in an increasingly digital world.
