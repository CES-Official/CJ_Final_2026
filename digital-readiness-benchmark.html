Digital Readiness Benchmark · Included with Season VII

Are your students AI ready — and digitally ready?

Artificial intelligence is changing how students learn, create and make decisions — but AI readiness is only part of the picture. Schools with 50 or more participating students in COMPUDON Junior Season VII receive a complimentary Digital Readiness Benchmark report, showing how their students perform across AI Literacy, Cyber Safety, Media Literacy and Critical Thinking.

Grades 3–9 Schools with 50+ participating students Complimentary School Report Principal Executive Summary

Give your students a meaningful digital-readiness experience, while your school gains measurable results, recognition, and national benchmarking.

What's included

A complete picture of where your students stand today

Every school with 50 or more participating students in COMPUDON Junior receives the same four deliverables, at no additional cost.

Grades 3–5 Benchmarking

Explorers-level students assessed on age-appropriate digital readiness indicators.

Grades 6–9 Benchmarking

Navigators-level students assessed on the same four readiness dimensions.

School-Level Report

Scored breakdown across AI Literacy, Cyber Safety, Media Literacy and Critical Thinking — with grade-wise detail.

Principal Executive Summary

A one-page summary built for leadership review, board reporting and CBSE compliance documentation.

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Participate

Students take part in COMPUDON Junior and contribute to the school's benchmark.

2

See the Results

The Digital Readiness Benchmark shows how participating students perform across AI Literacy, Cyber Safety, Media Literacy and Critical Thinking.

3

Receive Your Report

Your school-level report and Principal Executive Summary are shared within two weeks after the India Awards ceremony.

A clear, trusted benchmark. Actionable insights. Stronger digital readiness for every student.

On methodology: the Digital Readiness Benchmark is a new instrument for Season VII. Its scoring blueprint, sample papers, proctoring and integrity protocol, and appeals process are in development and will be published here before the assessment window opens. The Benchmark is not a diagnostic or psychometric assessment, and no score it produces should be read as a clinical or psychological measure of any child.

Better together

The Benchmark and the Championship work hand in hand

Together they give students both measurement and motivation — a clear picture of where they stand, and a goal worth striving towards. The Benchmark comes complimentary with championship participation.

Digital Readiness Benchmark

Measures where your students stand today across AI Literacy, Cyber Safety, Media Literacy and Critical Thinking — with the option to compare against other participating schools in Season VII. Complimentary for schools with 50+ participating students, shared privately with your school.

COMPUDON Junior Championship

Turns that baseline into motivation. An annual, skills-based competition with 100+ awards across school and national levels — giving students a goal to grow towards through the year.

Data Protection

Student Privacy & Data Security

Student information is handled with care, security, and appropriate safeguards at every stage of the Digital Readiness Benchmark.

Privacy-conscious data practices

We take student privacy into account when designing and operating the Benchmark.

Encryption

Data is protected using encryption where appropriate.

Limited data collection

We collect only what's required to score and report. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

School-controlled results

Results go to your school only. Nothing is published or shared externally without your explicit consent.
