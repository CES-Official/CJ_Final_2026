About the Founder

Pankaj Rai

Founder & President, COMPUDON Junior · CyberLearning Educational Society

Pankaj Rai is the Founder of COMPUDON and COMPUDON Junior, and the Managing Director of India CyberLearning. For more than 25 years, he has worked at the intersection of education, technology, and digital literacy, helping students and educators build the skills needed for an increasingly digital world.

His career has spanned leadership roles with international education initiatives across the United States, Asia, the Middle East, and the Caribbean before returning to India to establish one of the country's leading digital education organizations. As Certiport's exclusive partner, he established a network of over 1,000 testing centers for global certifications. As a Microsoft Global Training Partner, India CyberLearning has delivered large-scale digital skills and certification programs to schools, universities, governments, and leading organizations.

Recognizing that children now encounter AI, misinformation, online manipulation, cyber threats, and digital scams long before they are taught how to navigate them, he founded COMPUDON Junior with a simple mission: every child deserves structured preparation for the digital world, not just access to technology.

Over 100,000 students have participated in COMPUDON and COMPUDON Junior collectively, through 500+ schools and educational institutions. COMPUDON Junior is aligned with CBSE, NEP 2020, UNESCO, OECD, and the Microsoft Digital Literacy Standard, and is delivered by CyberLearning Educational Society (CES), a not-for-profit.

"We would never allow a child to drive on a highway without first learning the rules. Yet every day, we send children onto the digital highway with little or no preparation. That must change."
