1Program Overview

The COMPUDON Junior Student Ambassador Program recognises aspiring young leaders who inspire others, promote responsible digital citizenship, and help build a stronger community of digitally responsible students.

Founding Ambassadors are selected by COMPUDON Junior on the basis of achievement, participation, effort, enthusiasm, leadership potential, and a demonstrated interest in promoting responsible digital citizenship. Competition results are considered, but selection is not based on competition results alone.

2Eligibility Criteria

A candidate should meet the following:

  • Current or past COMPUDON Junior winner at the regional or national level. Non-winning students may be considered on school recommendation.
  • In Grade 6 or above at the time of appointment.
  • Shows communication, leadership, enthusiasm, and responsible digital citizenship.
  • Nominated by their school or by COMPUDON Junior.
3Appointment & Renewal

Initial Term: 15 August to 14 August of the following year

Applications & Renewal: Fresh enrolments and an annual review each July; renewal applications are due by 7 August each year

Maximum Tenure: Up to three (3) consecutive one-year terms

Renewal is subject to the following conditions:

  • Ambassador remains enrolled as a student in good academic standing
  • Satisfactory participation record (events, content, mentoring) for the term
  • No conduct violations or disciplinary actions during the term
  • Submission of the annual renewal application by 7 August
4Student Ambassador Expectations
  • Encourages and supports fellow students
  • Mentors at least one junior participant during the competition season
  • Creates or contributes to at least one piece of original video content each year
  • Communicates responsibly with the COMPUDON Junior team

These expectations do not apply during school examinations, pre-arranged school events, or other significant academic commitments. Student Ambassadors are expected to prioritize their education and well-being.

6Code of Conduct

Being a COMPUDON Junior Ambassador is an opportunity to lead, inspire, and have fun while making a positive difference. Ambassadors are expected to:

  • Be kind, respectful, and welcoming to all
  • Be honest and play fair
  • Use digital platforms responsibly and safely
  • Represent COMPUDON Junior positively
  • Treat fellow students, teachers, and event teams with respect
  • Complete assigned responsibilities within agreed timelines

Serious or repeated behavior that goes against these values may result in the Ambassador role being withdrawn.

iData Handling Notice

This notice explains how CyberLearning Educational Society (CES, Regd.), Regn. No. 53095/2005, registered office C-3/2, 1st Floor, Krishan Nagar, Delhi 110051, handles personal data collected through the Student Ambassador Program in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect and why: We collect only the details needed to assess the application, verify parental consent, and administer the Ambassador role — student name, class/grade, date of birth, school, city/state; parent/guardian name, relationship, and contact details; and the student's photograph (if appointed and where media consent is given).

No Tracking or Profiling (Section 9(3)): We do not track, behaviourally monitor, profile, or serve targeted advertising to children, and we do not sell or monetise student data.

Data Access: Restricted to authorized representatives of CyberLearning Educational Society (Regd.). Data is never sold or shared for marketing purposes.

Your Rights: You may review, update, or request deletion of your child's data, or withdraw consent, at any time. Withdrawing consent for media publication will remove your child's profile from our platforms.

Grievance Officer: To exercise any right or raise a concern, contact Ms. Shital Rai, Grievance Officer, at privacy@compudonjunior.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) under the DPDP Act, 2023.
