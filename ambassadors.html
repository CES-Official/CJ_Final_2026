1. Program Overview

The COMPUDON Junior Student Ambassador Program recognizes exceptional young achievers who demonstrate leadership, responsible digital citizenship and a passion for making a positive difference. Ambassadors inspire peers, represent COMPUDON Junior at events and activities, and help build a stronger community of digitally responsible young leaders across India.

2. Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet all of the following requirements to be considered for appointment:

  • Must be a current or past Winner at regional or national level
  • Enrolled as a student (Grade 6 and above, or equivalent) at the time of appointment
  • Demonstrates strong communication, leadership, and representational qualities
  • Nominated by a school authority or COMPUDON Junior
  • Reviewed and approved by the COMPUDON Junior Ambassador Selection Committee

Selection

Founding Ambassadors are selected by COMPUDON Junior based on a combination of achievement, participation, effort, enthusiasm, leadership potential, and demonstrated interest in promoting responsible digital citizenship. Competition results may be considered, but selection is not based solely on competition results. Appointment is subject to written parental consent.

3. Appointment & Renewal

Initial Term: One (1) year, commencing August 15 each year

Renewal Cycle: Annual review conducted each July

Maximum Tenure: Up to three (3) consecutive one-year terms

Term Start Date: August 15, 2026

Renewal is subject to the following conditions:

  • Ambassador remains enrolled as a student in good academic standing
  • Satisfactory participation record (events, content, mentoring) for the term
  • No conduct violations or disciplinary actions during the term
  • Submission of the annual renewal application by July 7

4. Student Ambassador Expectations

  • Represent COMPUDON Junior at school events, virtual events, or online activities organized by COMPUDON Junior, as reasonably available
  • Participate in the Annual Award Ceremony, where applicable
  • Support and encourage fellow students, including mentoring at least one junior participant during the competition season
  • Create or contribute to at least one piece of original content, such as a blog or video, during the year promoting digital literacy, cyber safety, or responsible digital citizenship
  • Stay responsive to reasonable communications from the COMPUDON Junior team

These expectations do not apply during school examinations, pre-arranged school events, or other significant academic commitments. Student Ambassadors are expected to prioritize their education and well-being.

6. Code of Conduct

Being a COMPUDON Junior Ambassador is an opportunity to lead, inspire, and have fun while making a positive difference. Ambassadors are expected to:

  • Be kind, respectful, and welcoming to others
  • Be honest and play fair
  • Use digital platforms responsibly and safely
  • Represent COMPUDON Junior positively
  • Treat fellow students, teachers, and event teams with respect

Serious or repeated behavior that goes against these values may result in the Ambassador role being withdrawn.

Data Handling Notice

This notice explains how CyberLearning Educational Society (Regd.) ("Data Fiduciary") and India CyberLearning Pvt. Ltd. ("Data Processor") handle personal data collected through the Student Ambassador Program in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Data We Collect: Student's name, class/grade, age, date of birth, school name, city/state; parent/guardian's name, relationship, contact details; student's photograph (if appointed); school representative's details.

Purpose: Evaluating eligibility, administering program communications, issuing certificates, and publishing profiles (subject to separate media consent).

Prohibition on Tracking & Profiling (Section 9(3)): We shall not engage in behavioral tracking, online activity monitoring, targeted advertising, or profiling directed at children.

Data Access: Restricted to authorized representatives of CyberLearning Educational Society (Regd.) and India CyberLearning Pvt. Ltd. Data will never be sold or shared for marketing purposes.

Security & Retention: Data is stored using access-controlled, encrypted digital platforms and retained for the appointment duration plus one year, then securely deleted.

Your Rights: Access, correct, or delete personal data; withdraw consent at any time (resulting in data deletion within 30 days). Contact our Privacy Officer, Ms. Shital Rai, at privacy@compudonjunior.com. If unsatisfied, you may file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).
