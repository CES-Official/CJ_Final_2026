1

Binding Agreement & Parental Consent

Participation in this Championship constitutes absolute acceptance of these Official Rules.

To participate in COMPUDON Junior Season VII (the “Championship”), the parent or legal guardian (“Parent/Guardian”) of the participant must agree to these Official Rules. Please read these Rules carefully prior to registration. Participation constitutes a binding legal agreement between the participant, their Parent/Guardian, and CyberLearning Educational Society (“Organizer” or “CES”). If you do not agree to these Rules, you may not register or participate in the Championship.

2

Championship Eligibility

To be eligible to participate, a candidate must:

  • Complete a qualifying Level 1 assessment within the designated testing window published on the official website.
  • Be between the ages of 8–14 years as of August 15, 2026.
  • Have explicit, verifiable consent from a Parent/Guardian to participate.
  • Be enrolled in a recognized academic school or educational institution.
3

Championship Period & Format

Testing Windows: For exact schedules, testing windows, and deadline updates, please refer to the Key Dates section on the homepage.

Modes of Entry

  • School Registration (Preferred): Facilitated directly through the candidate's school under a designated School Coordinator.
  • Individual Registration: Eligible candidates whose schools are not directly participating may register individually via authorized platform channels, subject to verification by the Organizer.

Championship Stages

  • Stage 1 — National Qualifying Round (Level 1): Conducted within participating schools or supervised online windows.
  • Stage 2 — India Finals (Level 2): Top-qualifying candidates from Level 1 across age categories are invited to compete in the National Finals.
  • Stage 3 — International Recognition: Top national podium winners in participating countries will receive International Recognition, as applicable, subject to participant thresholds across participating countries.

Language: All assessments and communications are administered strictly in English.

4

Qualification & Tie-Breaker Criteria

  • Qualification: Shortlisting from Level 1 to Level 2 (India Finals) is based strictly on the highest score achieved on the Level 1 assessment.
  • Tie-Breaker Rule: In the event of tied scores, the candidate who completed the assessment in the shortest amount of time (as recorded automatically by the assessment platform engine) will be ranked higher.
  • Timed Assessments: Assessments are timed automatically. Upon expiry of the allotted time, the testing platform automatically submits the exam. Incomplete or altered submissions are void.
5

Winner Determination & Awards

  • Podium Positions: Following the India Finals, the highest-scoring candidate in each age category will be declared the National Champion (Gold Medalist). The second and third highest scores will be declared First Runner-Up (Silver Medalist) and Second Runner-Up (Bronze Medalist), respectively.
  • Regional & Institutional Recognition: Regional Champions, Merit List Rank Holders, and School Top Performers are determined based on standardized score and time metrics.
  • Notification: Winners and their school coordinators will be notified via official email, SMS, or phone contact details provided during registration.
  • Award Terms: Recognition, trophies, badges, and prizes are non-transferable, non-substitutable, and carry no cash equivalents unless explicitly stated by the Organizer.
6

Data Protection & Privacy (DPDP Act Compliance)

Participant privacy and child data security are strictly enforced in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act:

  • Specified Purpose & Processing: Personal data (including student name, date of birth, grade, school, parent email, and phone number) is processed solely to administer the Championship, issue diagnostic readiness reports, deliver certificates, and verify award eligibility.
  • Protection of Minors: All processing of minor candidates' data is performed pursuant to verifiable consent provided by the Parent/Guardian. The Organizer strictly refrains from behavioral tracking, profiling, or targeted commercial advertising directed at minor participants.
  • Data Principal Rights: A Parent/Guardian retains the right to access, review, rectify, or request deletion of their child's personal data, or withdraw consent, by submitting a written request to our Grievance Redressal Officer at privacy@compudonjunior.com.
  • Third-Party Security: Student data is stored securely using standard encryption protocols and will never be sold, rented, or commercialized to third parties.
7

Publicity Release

By participating, the participant and their Parent/Guardian grant the Organizer and its authorized affiliates a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use the participant's name, school name, city/state, photograph, and video footage (if applicable) in official Hall of Fame archives, annual impact reports, website display, and official press releases without additional notice or financial compensation, except where prohibited by law.

8

Taxes & Statutory Obligations

All prizes or awards provided are subject to applicable Indian tax laws and statutory withholding requirements (if any). All tax liabilities, filings, and statutory compliance arising from the receipt of any prize or award are the sole responsibility of the winning participant's Parent/Guardian.

9

Indemnity & Limitation of Liability

  • Indemnity: The participant and their Parent/Guardian agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless CyberLearning Educational Society, India CyberLearning Pvt. Ltd., its directors, officers, employees, and execution partners from and against any claims, losses, liabilities, damages, or expenses (including legal fees) arising out of any breach of these Rules, academic dishonesty, or violation of third-party rights by the participant.
  • Limitation of Liability: To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Organizer shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, platform technical glitches, internet latency, power outages, or loss of submission data outside the direct control of the testing server infrastructure.
10

General Conditions, Code of Conduct & Force Majeure

  • Code of Conduct: The Organizer reserves the absolute right to disqualify any participant who attempts to tamper with the assessment engine, uses unauthorized aids, engages in fraud, or acts in a manner disruptive to the Championship.
  • School Qualification Thresholds: To maintain broad regional diversity, the Organizer reserves the right to limit the total number of candidates qualifying for the India Finals from any single institution.
  • Force Majeure: If the Championship or any associated event cannot proceed as planned due to acts of God, public health emergencies, governmental orders, cyber-attacks, or technical disasters beyond reasonable control, the Organizer reserves the right to suspend, modify, or cancel the event. In the event of cancellation, the Organizer's sole obligation shall be to offer affected eligible participants free entry into the subsequent season's championship.
11

Governing Law & Arbitration

  • Governing Law: These Rules shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
  • Arbitration: Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or in connection with the Championship or these Rules shall be submitted to binding arbitration before a sole arbitrator appointed mutually by the parties in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act.
  • Jurisdiction: The seat and venue of arbitration shall be New Delhi, India. Courts in New Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matters arising from these Rules.
12

Amendments

The Organizer reserves the right to modify, alter, or update these Official Rules at its sole discretion to preserve administrative fairness and security. Any amendments will be published immediately on the official website (compudonjunior.com) and shall be binding on all participants.
