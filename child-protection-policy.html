Child Protection, Safeguarding & Event Safety Policy

COMPUDON Junior is committed to providing a safe, respectful and secure environment for every participating student, whether the activity is conducted online or in person.

Effective: 1 August 2026 Version: 1.0 Approved by: Managing Committee, CES Next Review: 31 July 2027
This Policy forms part of the safeguarding governance framework of CyberLearning Educational Society (Regd.) and should be read together with the COMPUDON Junior Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and the consent and declaration forms referenced in the Appendices below.

1

Purpose

COMPUDON Junior is committed to providing a safe, respectful and secure environment for all participating students. This Policy establishes the safeguarding principles, responsibilities and procedures applicable to all COMPUDON Junior activities, whether conducted online or in person.

2

Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • All participating students.
  • Parents and guardians.
  • Participating schools and coordinators.
  • Employees, consultants, judges, volunteers and event personnel.
  • National, regional and other physical events organized by COMPUDON Junior.
  • Online registrations, assessments and related activities.
3

Guiding Principles

COMPUDON Junior shall:

  • Place the safety and well-being of every student as a priority.
  • Treat every student with dignity, fairness and respect.
  • Maintain a safe and inclusive environment free from abuse, harassment, bullying and discrimination.
  • Protect the privacy and confidentiality of student information.
  • Promptly address safeguarding concerns.
4

Background Verification

All employees, consultants, volunteers, judges and event personnel having direct interaction with students during physical events shall undergo appropriate identity verification and background verification before assignment to such roles.

5

Code of Conduct

Personnel associated with COMPUDON Junior shall:

  • Behave professionally at all times.
  • Treat every student respectfully and without discrimination.
  • Avoid one-to-one isolated interaction with students wherever reasonably possible.
  • Avoid unnecessary physical contact.
  • Not request or exchange personal contact details with students.
  • Not communicate privately with students through personal social media or messaging platforms.
  • Immediately report any safeguarding concern.
6

Parent and School Responsibilities

Participating schools and parents shall:

  • Ensure the accuracy of student information submitted.
  • Obtain all required parental permissions.
  • Inform COMPUDON Junior of any medical conditions or special assistance requirements.
  • Ensure that students comply with event instructions.
7

Student Supervision

Students attending physical events shall remain under the supervision of:

  • Their parent or legal guardian; or
  • An authorized school representative.

COMPUDON Junior personnel shall not assume guardianship or custodial responsibility for any participating student.

8

Consent Requirements

Participation in physical events may require:

  • Parent/guardian consent.
  • Medical information declaration.
  • Emergency contact details.
  • Authorized chaperone declaration.
  • Photography and media consent.
9

Photography and Media

Photographs, videos, names or achievements of participating students shall be published only where appropriate consent has been obtained.

Parents or eligible participants may withdraw publication consent by submitting a written request. Reasonable efforts shall be made to remove such content from COMPUDON Junior-controlled platforms within a reasonable period.

10

Event Safety

For physical events, COMPUDON Junior shall, as appropriate:

  • Register participants upon arrival.
  • Restrict access to authorized persons.
  • Maintain emergency contact information.
  • Provide access to first aid arrangements.
  • Maintain emergency evacuation procedures.
  • Record and respond to safety incidents.
11

Event Insurance

COMPUDON Junior shall obtain appropriate event insurance for National Finals and such other physical events as may be determined necessary. Details of such coverage may be shared with participating institutions upon request.

12

Medical Emergencies

In the event of a medical emergency:

  • Immediate assistance shall be arranged.
  • Parents, guardians or the authorized school representative shall be informed as soon as reasonably practicable.
  • Emergency medical services shall be contacted whenever considered necessary.
  • An incident record shall be maintained.
13

Online Safety and Privacy

Student information shall be collected, processed and protected in accordance with the COMPUDON Junior Privacy Policy.

COMPUDON Junior shall:

  • Implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.
  • Restrict access to authorized personnel.
  • Never sell student personal data.
  • Use third-party service providers only for legitimate service delivery under appropriate contractual safeguards.
14

Reporting Safeguarding Concerns

Any person may report concerns relating to:

  • Child safety.
  • Abuse or harassment.
  • Bullying.
  • Inappropriate conduct.
  • Safety incidents.

Reports may be submitted to: support@compudonjunior.com

All reports shall be treated confidentially and reviewed promptly.

15

Policy Violations

Violation of this Policy may result in:

  • Removal from the event.
  • Disqualification.
  • Restriction from future participation.
  • Termination of engagement of personnel or volunteers.
  • Reporting to appropriate authorities where required by law.
16

Review

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically and may be amended by COMPUDON Junior whenever considered necessary.
