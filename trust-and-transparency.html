Trust & Transparency

Clear answers to the questions school leaders and parents ask before choosing a digital citizenship programme.

We built COMPUDON Junior on a simple belief: a programme that asks schools to trust it with their students should be completely open about what it costs, what it does with data, and what it delivers. Below are the questions we hear most often - and straight answers.

Fees & participation

What it costs, and how it works

What does COMPUDON Junior cost, and what is included?

Schools pay a single, transparent per-student fee that covers the full programme: curriculum access, the championship assessment, individual student reports, school-level Digital Readiness Reports, certificates, and badges.

There are no additional book purchases, no paid "Level 2" rounds, and no hidden upgrade fees. The exact fee is shared directly with school administrators during onboarding. Parents are never asked to pay separately.

Is participation compulsory for students?

Participation in COMPUDON Junior is always voluntary. We do not instruct or incentivise schools to make registration compulsory. Schools receive no per-student commission or financial kickback for registrations.

Our partnership is with the school as an institution choosing to invest in digital readiness - not a per-head transaction.

Are there extra books or materials parents need to buy?

No. All learning content and assessment material is included within the programme fee paid by the school. There are no mandatory workbooks, previous-year-paper packs, or paid preparation courses. Students prepare through the curriculum delivered during school hours.

What is the refund policy?

If a registered school is unable to participate due to unforeseen circumstances, we offer a full credit toward the next season or a pro-rata refund for students who have not accessed any programme materials.

Refund requests should be sent to connect@compudonjunior.com within 30 days of registration. Individual cases are handled promptly and fairly.

What schools & teachers can expect

Certificates, badges, and admin effort

What do the certificates and badges actually certify?

COMPUDON Junior badges are criterion-referenced, meaning they certify demonstrated skill mastery in Cyber Safety, Critical Thinking, Media Literacy, and AI Awareness - not an inflated rank number.

A student earns a Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum Compass Badge based on what they can actually do, not where they placed in a bell curve. We do not issue certificates with meaningless "International Rank 4,520 out of 50,000" numbers.

How much admin work does this create for teachers?

Minimal. School registration takes under 15 minutes using our bulk-upload template. We handle student communications, assessment logistics, report generation, and certificate dispatch.

The coordinating teacher's role is limited to scheduling the assessment window and distributing materials. We do not expect teachers to act as fee collectors, sales agents, or invigilators for an external exam.

Is the programme stressful for younger children (Grades 3-5)?

The Explorers category (Grades 3-5) is designed as a learning experience, not a high-stakes competition. Content uses stories, rhymes, and age-appropriate scenarios.

The assessment is formative - it identifies what children know and where they need support, not where they rank against thousands of peers. We do not publish individual rankings for this age group. The goal is awareness and confidence, not pressure. All content is reviewed by a practicing neuropsychologist for age-appropriateness and psychological safety.

Data, privacy & grievances

How we protect you

What happens with student data after the programme?

COMPUDON Junior is fully aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Student data is collected only for programme delivery and reporting.

We never sell, share, or monetise student or parent data. We do not cold-call families or send promotional messages after the programme. Schools and parents can request data deletion at any time by writing to privacy@compudonjunior.com.

How do I raise a concern or complaint?

Our designated Grievance Officer, Ms. Shital Rai, can be reached at privacy@compudonjunior.com. For programme-related queries, write to connect@compudonjunior.com or call +91 83830 56545.

We respond to all concerns within five working days.
