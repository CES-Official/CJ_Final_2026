For Parents

Helping Families Navigate the Digital World

No generation of parents has had to raise children in a world quite like this one. AI, social media, online gaming, and an endless stream of digital content have become part of everyday childhood. What children often need most is not another rule, but a trusted adult who is willing to listen, ask questions, and learn alongside them.

Our Parent Guides are designed to support those conversations — each exploring a real-world digital challenge in clear, practical language, helping parents build confidence rather than expertise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered

What age group is COMPUDON Junior for?

Children aged 8–14 (Grades 3–12 equivalent), split into the Explorers (Grades 3–5) and Navigators (Grades 6–9) categories.

What will my child learn?

Your child learns to stay safe online, think critically about digital content and misinformation, use AI tools responsibly, and build confident, safe digital habits — skills reinforced through stories, exercises and a benchmarked assessment.

Can my child register individually, without a school?

Yes, whether or not the school is participating. Students from participating schools register at ₹699; direct individual registration is ₹1,499 - see the Students page for details.

Is my child's data protected?

Yes. COMPUDON Junior's data handling is designed around the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, with details set out in our Privacy Policy and Child Safety & Data page.
