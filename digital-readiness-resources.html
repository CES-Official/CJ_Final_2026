Curated Reading

Explore by Topic

A selection of research and reporting from leading organizations, organized around the four areas COMPUDON Junior develops.

AI & Children

Education Week Lessons on AI for Elementary Students: "Teach Them Good Habits Now" Supports the idea that AI literacy should begin early, with age-appropriate instruction about how AI works and its limitations — especially relevant for Grades 3–5. UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students A global reference setting out the knowledge, skills and values students need to understand and use AI responsibly — the foundation for age-appropriate AI education. UNICEF Responsible AI for Children and Young People UNICEF guidance on protecting children's rights and safety as AI becomes part of their everyday lives.

Media Literacy

UNESCO Shaping a New Generation: Integrating Media & Information Literacy in India's Education System India-specific discussion of young people's exposure to digital environments and the need to understand disinformation and manipulated content. UNESCO Media & Information Literate Citizens: Think Critically, Click Wisely UNESCO's flagship curriculum on helping learners question, verify and responsibly navigate the information they encounter online. OECD Policy Responses to False and Misleading Digital Content Examines how misinformation spreads online and what education and policy can do to build resilience against it.

Cyber Safety

Education Week Parents Don't Hate Ed Tech. They Just Want More Balanced Use Reports that most parents want children taught to use AI effectively — alongside transparency, safety, and protection of children's data. UNICEF Children Are Adopting AI More Than Three Times Faster Than Adults A quarter of children surveyed feared their images being manipulated into deepfakes — a reminder that many AI systems reach children with no safety guardrails. ITU Child Online Protection The UN agency for digital technologies sets out global guidance for keeping children safe, secure and empowered online.

Critical Thinking

Education Week 3 Ways Teachers Can Help Students Think Critically About AI Practical strategies to help students examine AI-generated content and avoid being misled by inaccurate or inappropriate material. UNESCO Media & Information Literacy and Digital Competencies Connects media literacy with critical thinking, ethical AI use, misinformation, and AI literacy across a single framework. OECD Empowering Learners for the Age of AI Explores the competencies — including critical thinking and judgment — students need to thrive alongside AI. OECD PISA 2029: Media & Artificial Intelligence Literacy The OECD's global assessment will measure how well students evaluate media and AI — signalling how central these skills have become.

External articles are provided for informational purposes. COMPUDON Junior is not affiliated with or endorsed by these organizations. Links open on the publisher's own website.
