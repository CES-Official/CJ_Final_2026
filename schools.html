Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered

What does a school get by registering for COMPUDON Junior?

Schools receive complete study materials, an assessment platform, student guides, a school dashboard with real-time progress visibility, benchmarked readiness reports, and a School Future Readiness Report that leadership can present to boards and parents.

How much work does this add for teachers?

Minimal. Complete study materials, the assessment platform and student guides are provided - teachers coordinate participation rather than creating content or marking assessments.
