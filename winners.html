Champions Through the Seasons

Every season, students compete to demonstrate their knowledge of cyber safety, media literacy, AI literacy, and critical thinking. Here we recognize students whose achievement, performance, and dedication have earned them national, regional, and international recognition.

Behind every champion is a school that made digital citizenship a priority.Season VII registrations are open. Give your students the platform to learn the critical skills for life and also represent your school on the national stage.
