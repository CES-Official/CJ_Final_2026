Why Return

Why Schools Continue with COMPUDON Junior

Track Year-on-Year Growth

Returning schools receive comparative performance data across seasons, showing how student digital readiness improves over time.

Regional & National Recognition

Students compete for awards across five regions and at the national level. Your school's name appears on the COMPUDON Junior Hall of Fame.

CBSE-Aligned Curriculum

Season VII content maps directly to CBSE Skill-81/2025 and Acad-39/2026 circulars — fulfilling curriculum requirements through participation.

Priority Coordinator Support

Returning school coordinators receive dedicated onboarding, faster response times, and access to the coordinator resource library.

School Future Readiness Report

Returning schools receive a branded institutional report assessing digital readiness across student cohorts — available after Level 1.

Updated Season VII Content

New modules on AI Literacy, Responsible AI, and Media Literacy join the core curriculum — ensuring students learn what matters now.
