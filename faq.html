Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the questions school leaders, teachers, and parents frequently ask.

About the programme

General questions

What is COMPUDON Junior?

COMPUDON Junior is India's National Championship on AI Awareness, Media Literacy, Cyber Safety & Critical Thinking for school students aged 8-14. It combines a structured study program with a benchmarked assessment and a national recognition pathway, operated by CyberLearning Educational Society (CES) and powered by a Microsoft Global Training Partner.

What age group is COMPUDON Junior for?

Children aged 8-14 as of August 15, 2026 (Grades 3-9), split into Explorers (Grades 3-5) and Navigators (Grades 6-9) categories, each with age-appropriate learning resources and assessments.

What will my child learn?

Your child learns to stay safe online, think critically about digital content and misinformation, use AI tools responsibly, and build confident, safe digital habits - skills reinforced through stories, exercises and a benchmarked assessment.

How can students participate?

Two ways. If your child's school is participating, they register through the school at the special school-participant fee of ₹699. If the school is not participating, a parent can register the child directly at ₹1,499.

Can parents register their child directly?

Yes. Parents can register a child directly whether or not the school is participating. Students from participating schools receive the lower ₹699 fee; direct individual registration is ₹1,499. See the Students page for details.

Fees & transparency

What it costs - and what you get

What does Season VII cost, and what is included?

Season VII participation is ₹699 per student through a participating school (₹1,499 for direct individual registration), paid only once. This covers curriculum and learning resources, practice exams, the Level 1 examination, the Level 2/India Final examination if qualified, and prizes and recognition. There are no additional book purchases and no hidden upgrade fees. Optional preparation material may be offered ahead of the Level 2 round, but it is not required for participation or qualification. Parents are never asked to pay separately.

Is participation compulsory for students?

No. Participation in COMPUDON Junior is always voluntary. We do not instruct or incentivise schools to make registration compulsory. Schools receive no per-student commission or financial kickback for registrations. Our partnership is with the school as an institution choosing to invest in digital readiness - not a per-head transaction.

Are there extra books or materials parents need to buy?

No. All learning content and assessment material is included within the programme fee paid by the school. There are no mandatory workbooks, previous-year-paper packs, or paid preparation courses. Students prepare through the curriculum delivered during school hours.

What is the refund policy?

If a registered school is unable to participate due to unforeseen circumstances, we offer a full credit toward the next season or a pro-rata refund for students who have not accessed any programme materials. Refund requests should be sent to connect@compudonjunior.com within 30 days of registration. Individual cases are handled promptly and fairly.

For schools & teachers

What schools need to know

What does a school get by registering?

Schools receive complete study materials, an assessment platform, student guides, a school dashboard with real-time progress visibility, benchmarked readiness reports, and a School Future Readiness Report that leadership can present to boards and parents.

How much admin work does this create for teachers?

Minimal. In our experience, less than 10 hours across the championship. We handle parent and student communication, provide access to the Learning Management System, assessment logistics, report generation, and all other activities. The coordinating teacher's role, as we see it, is limited to ensuring parents and students know about this and register, scheduling the assessment window, and ensuring students write the exam within the appointed time. We do not expect teachers to act as fee collectors, sales agents, or invigilators for an external exam.

Can a school track improvement over time?

Yes. Students are benchmarked at entry and tracked across readiness dimensions, and reporting is built to show change over successive seasons.

What documentation does a school receive for compliance records?

Schools receive a School Future Readiness Report and benchmarked student reporting that can be used directly as supporting documentation for CBSE and NEP compliance records.

CBSE & NEP alignment

Compliance and curriculum mapping

Is COMPUDON Junior aligned with CBSE requirements?

Yes. The program is mapped to NEP 2020 and supports schools in delivering CBSE Circular Skill-81/2025 and Acad-39/2026, with ready-made documentation for compliance and future-readiness records.

Which CBSE circulars does the programme align with?

The program is mapped to CBSE Circular Skill-81/2025 and Acad-39/2026, alongside NEP 2020, and supports schools in meeting these requirements with ready-made documentation.

Does the program support NEP 2020 reporting requirements?

Yes. NEP 2020 mandates report cards reflecting both academic and non-academic achievement, including creativity and critical thinking. COMPUDON Junior's benchmarked readiness reporting is designed to support this.

Certificates & recognition

What badges and certificates mean

What do the certificates and badges actually certify?

COMPUDON Junior badges are criterion-referenced, meaning they certify demonstrated skill mastery in Cyber Safety, Critical Thinking, Media Literacy, and AI Awareness - not an inflated rank number. A student earns a Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum Compass Badge based on what they can actually do, not where they placed in a bell curve. We do not issue certificates with meaningless "International Rank 4,520 out of 50,000" numbers.

Is the programme stressful for younger children (Grades 3-5)?

The Explorers category (Grades 3-5) is designed as a learning experience, not a high-stakes competition. Content uses stories, rhymes, and age-appropriate scenarios. The assessment is formative - it identifies what children know and where they need support, not where they rank against thousands of peers. We do not publish individual rankings for this age group. The goal is awareness and confidence, not pressure.

Data, privacy & grievances

How we protect you

Is my child's data protected?

Yes. COMPUDON Junior's data handling is designed around the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Student data is collected only for programme delivery and reporting. We never sell, share, or monetise student or parent data. We do not cold-call families or send promotional messages after the programme. Schools and parents can request data deletion at any time by writing to privacy@compudonjunior.com.

How do I raise a concern or complaint?

Our designated Grievance Officer, Ms. Shital Rai, can be reached at privacy@compudonjunior.com. For programme-related queries, write to connect@compudonjunior.com or call +91 83830 56545. We respond to all concerns within five working days.
