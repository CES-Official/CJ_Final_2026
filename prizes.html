Awards & Recognition

Recognition at every level of achievement

COMPUDON Junior Season VII recognizes outstanding achievement through trophies, medals, gifts, certificates, digital badges, and national recognition. All awards are presented separately for each competition category — Explorers (Grades 3-5) and Navigators (Grades 6-9) — ensuring students compete with peers of a similar age.

1

India Champion

Championship Trophy, Gold Medal, 1st Place Gifts, a feature on the COMPUDON Junior Wall of Fame, and international recognition.

2

National 1st Runner-up · Rank 2

Silver Trophy & Medal, 2nd Place Gifts, Achievement Certificate, and recognition on the COMPUDON Junior website with photograph.

3

National 2nd Runner-up · Rank 3

Bronze Trophy & Medal, 3rd Place Gifts, Achievement Certificate, and recognition on the COMPUDON Junior website with photograph.

4–10

National Top 10

Students ranked 4th to 10th nationally receive a Medal, Achievement Certificate, and recognition on the COMPUDON Junior website with photograph.

11–100

National Honor Roll

Students ranked 11th to 100th nationally receive a Medal, Achievement Certificate, and name recognition on the COMPUDON Junior website.

School Awards

Category-wise top 3 winners are recognized, and top-performing schools are honoured at both the Regional and National level.

Every Participant

Every student who takes part receives a Digital Certificate of Participation.

International Recognition

The India Champion in each category earns the opportunity to represent COMPUDON Junior on the international stage — carrying their achievement, and their school's name, beyond national borders. As international participation grows each season, so does the platform for India's finest young digital citizens.
